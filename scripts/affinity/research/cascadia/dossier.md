## 1. Supporters

### Portland Timbers
- Timbers Army founded 2001 (as "Cascade Rangers", 8 founders) in the USL era; renamed 2002; reported 5,000+ members (date of count not given). [Wikipedia: Timbers Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timbers_Army)
- 107 Independent Supporters Trust (107IST, est. 2010, named after section 107) is the non-profit umbrella for the Timbers Army and the Thorns' Rose City Riveters. [Wikipedia: Timbers Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timbers_Army); [107IST](https://107ist.org/timbersarmy)
- Current 107IST membership count: not published in anything found. **[UNVERIFIED]** [107IST FAQ](https://107ist.org/107ist/membership-faq)
- Section: North End, sections 101-108 and 201-208, general admission, standing, drums, flags, smoke, constant chanting. [RateYourSeats](https://www.rateyourseats.com/providence-park/seating/timbers-army-sections); [Wikipedia: Timbers Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timbers_Army)
- 19 July 2025 (50th anniversary, v Minnesota): Timbers Army displayed what the club and MLS call the largest painted tifo in MLS history, covering the north end, most of the west side, plus elements on the east and south. [Timbers.com](https://www.timbers.com/news/timbers-army-unveils-largest-painted-tifo-in-mls-history-on-tea-s-50th-anniversary-night); [MLSsoccer.com](https://www.mlssoccer.com/news/must-see-portland-timbers-unveil-largest-painted-tifo-in-mls-history)
- Final home match of 2025 (v San Diego): banner "WITHOUT THEM THERE IS NO US" with flags of the players' home countries, reported as a response to 2025 immigration enforcement. [Yahoo Sports](https://sports.yahoo.com/article/portland-timbers-just-clinched-tifo-201224451.html)
- 2009 tifo of Timber Jim chainsawing the Space Needle (USL era) later named one of the five best tifos in MLS history. [Wikipedia: Timbers Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timbers_Army)
- Attendance: every MLS home match sold out from the first match (14 Apr 2011); 100th consecutive regular-season sellout reached in the mid-2010s. [KGW](https://www.kgw.com/article/sports/soccer/timbers/timbers-sellout-100th-consecutive-regular-season-game-at-providence-park/283-316515481)
- The exact date the sellout streak ended is not documented in anything found; 2022 average (23,341) was below the 25,218 capacity. **[UNVERIFIED end date]** [Wikipedia: 2022 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Portland_Timbers_season)
- Season-ticket waitlist: 13,000+ around 2017; 12,000+ in 2018; the 2019 expansion (+~4,000 seats) cut it substantially; a 2024 source cites 10,000+ and <2% annual turnover. [Search summary of Habitat/Portland Mercury sources](https://habitatportlandregion.org/timbers-season-tickets/); [Portland Mercury 2022](https://www.portlandmercury.com/timbers/2022/02/07/38259131/demand-for-timbers-season-tickets-is-down-and-there-are-several-reasons-why) (2024 figure **[UNVERIFIED]**)
- Average attendance: 2022 23,341; 2023 23,102; 2024 21,949; 2025 22,134 (low 11,515); 2026 to date ~22,265 across 14 home matches (range 19,351-24,686; three matches at the listed 24,686). [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Portland_Timbers_season); [2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Portland_Timbers_season); [2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Portland_Timbers_season); [2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Portland_Timbers_season); [2026](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Portland_Timbers_season)
- Away following: 107IST runs organised Timbers Army trips to away matches (2025 listings include Nashville, Colorado, LA Galaxy, RSL, LAFC, Houston, Seattle, San Diego). [107IST event: @Seattle 4 Oct 2025](https://107ist.org/event-6169580); [@San Diego 26 Oct 2025](https://107ist.org/event-6408911)
- Bad-season behaviour: 2021-22 supporters kept buying tickets but boycotted merchandise and concessions to press for reforms after the Thorns abuse reports. [OPB](https://www.opb.org/article/2021/10/06/portland-thorns-timbers-partial-fan-boycott-amid-sex-abuse-scandal/); [KGW](https://www.kgw.com/article/news/local/timbers-and-thorns-fan-groups-announce-boycott-after-sexual-harassment-allegations/283-fa1b50fc-f5ce-4659-8f4e-7f9918fbd655)
- Conflict with club: Jan 2022 the front office halted monthly meetings with 107IST; 4 Oct 2022 Timbers Army and Riveters demanded the removal of Wilkinson and Golub and that Paulson sell; "Sell The Team" signs appeared in 2022 and 2023. [Stumptown Footy](https://www.stumptownfooty.com/2022/1/14/22884687/timbers-thorns-fc-has-permanently-halted-monthly-meetings-with-107ist); [Portland Mercury](https://www.portlandmercury.com/news/2022/10/04/46114079/thorns-timbers-fan-groups-sever-ties-with-club-management-over-devastating-report); [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/soccer/story/_/id/46491142/portland-thorns-rebuild-new-owners-raj-sports-bhathal-replace-paulson)
- Re-engagement: 9 Nov 2023 the front office (CEO Heather Davis, GM Ned Grabavoy, new coach Phil Neville) met the Timbers Army steering committee and 107IST board. [107IST blog](https://107ist.org/TimbersArmyBlog/13282792)
- Iron Front 2019: MLS banned the anti-fascist Iron Front symbol as "political"; the Timbers Army stayed silent for the first 33 minutes of an August match (for 1933); MLS reversed on 24 Sep 2019. [Wikipedia: Timbers Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timbers_Army)
- Identity: supporter culture is openly anti-fascist and left-leaning (Iron Front campaign, 2025 immigrant-solidarity banner). [Wikipedia: Timbers Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timbers_Army); [Yahoo Sports](https://sports.yahoo.com/article/portland-timbers-just-clinched-tifo-201224451.html)
- Reputation: several 2025 stadium rankings list Providence Park first in MLS for atmosphere. [Football Ground Map](https://www.footballgroundmap.com/articles/ranking-the-2025-standouts-mls-stadiums-you-should-visit-this-year)

### Seattle Sounders FC
- Emerald City Supporters (ECS) founded 2005 (USL era): <100 members then, ~600 at the first MLS match (Mar 2009), ~2,700 paid by 2012, ~5,000 by 2017 (latest figure found). [Wikipedia: ECS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emerald_City_Supporters); [Wikipedia: Sounders supporters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle_Sounders_FC_supporters)
- ECS sit in sections 121-123 (south end, "Brougham End"); March to the Match from Occidental Park, led by the Sound Wave marching band. [Wikipedia: ECS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emerald_City_Supporters); [Wikipedia: Sounders supporters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle_Sounders_FC_supporters)
- Other groups: Gorilla FC (section 120, describes itself as anti-racist, anti-sexist, anti-homophobic), North End Faithful (2009), Eastside Supporters (2010, family oriented), La Barra Fuerza Verde (Latino sub-group of ECS). [Wikipedia: Sounders supporters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle_Sounders_FC_supporters); [Wikipedia: ECS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emerald_City_Supporters)
- Sept 2025: ECS and La Barra Fuerza Verde displayed "¡Todos Somos Sounders!" tifo; ECS statement condemned immigration raids ("ripping families apart ... is unacceptable"). [ECS](https://weareecs.com/2025/09/13/somos-sounders-tifo-display/)
- March 2026: Concacaf barred ECS banners (Iron Front, "Anti-Fascist | Anti-Racist | Always Seattle", BLM, trans-rights, raised fist) at the Champions Cup match in Spokane; ECS turned it into a fundraiser for LGBTQ+ youth, Black community and immigrant-solidarity groups, with front-office support. [Sounder at Heart](https://www.sounderatheart.com/2026/03/concacaf-takes-a-stand-against-solidarity/)
- Iron Front 2019: in July, a letter from Hanauer and COO Bart Wiley reprimanded supporters and equated the Iron Front with the Proud Boys; ECS walked out after a leader was ejected; the club later apologised publicly; MLS lifted the ban. [Seattle Times (apology)](https://www.seattletimes.com/sports/sounders/sounders-apologize-to-fan-groups-for-banning-iron-front-flag-but-tension-persists/); [Seattle Times (walkout)](https://www.seattletimes.com/sports/sounders/sounders-supporters-group-walks-out-during-game-to-protest-ejection-of-leader-for-displaying-iron-front-flag/)
- 2023: ECS sent an open letter to all owners opposing the Providence shirt deal. [Seattle Times](https://www.seattletimes.com/sports/sounders/sounders-partnership-with-providence-draws-ire-of-fans/)
- Attendance: MLS highs of ~44,000 (2013-2015) and 43,666 (2017); 2022 33,607; 2023 32,161 (3rd in MLS); 2024 30,754; 2025 30,993 (2nd lowest in club history, still ~30% below the 2017 peak); 2026 to date ~31,700 over 13 home matches (range 28,649-38,287). [List of seasons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Seattle_Sounders_FC_seasons); [2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Seattle_Sounders_FC_season); [2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Seattle_Sounders_FC_season); [Sounder at Heart](https://www.sounderatheart.com/2025/10/viewership-numbers-are-a-mixed-bag-for-mls-sounders/); [2026](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Seattle_Sounders_FC_season)
- Record single MLS crowd for the rivalry: 66,452 v Portland, 7 Oct 2012. [Wikipedia: rivalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portland_Timbers%E2%80%93Seattle_Sounders_rivalry)
- Season tickets: renewal rate historically >90%; in July 2026 a Sounder at Heart writer reported more people telling him they would not renew than in any year he could remember (anecdotal). [Sounder at Heart](https://www.sounderatheart.com/2026/07/everything-you-need-to-know-about-season-ticket-renewals/)
- No waitlist mentioned on the membership page; tickets generally available. [SoundersFC.com](https://www.soundersfc.com/tickets/membership)
- Away following: ECS sells organised away-section tickets (e.g., at Portland 17 May 2025). [ECS](https://weareecs.com/product/away-vs-portland-2025-05-17/)
- Bad-season behaviour 2026: fan frustration centred on low transfer spending after the 5-1 home loss to Portland; no organised protests documented in the sources found. [Sounder at Heart, 21 Jul 2026](https://www.sounderatheart.com/2026/07/sounders-leadership-needs-to-listen-to-fed-up-fans/)
- Reputation: Lumen Field ranked 2nd to Providence Park in a 2025 MLS stadium ranking ("wall of noise" when full). [Football Ground Map](https://www.footballgroundmap.com/articles/ranking-the-2025-standouts-mls-stadiums-you-should-visit-this-year)

## 2. Ground

### Portland Timbers
- Providence Park opened 1926 (Multnomah Stadium, later Civic Stadium, PGE Park, JELD-WEN Field); site used since 1893; 2026 is its centenary. [Wikipedia: Providence Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Providence_Park); [Timbers.com](https://www.timbers.com/news/timbers-reveal-civic-stadium-kit-honoring-100th-anniversary-of-providence-park)
- Owned by the City of Portland, operated by Paulson's Peregrine Sports under a 25-year agreement to 2035. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Providence_Park); [Portland.gov annual report](https://www.portland.gov/venues/overview/2024-2025-annual-report)
- Soccer-specific since the 2011 conversion; 2019 east-side expansion (~$75m private) took capacity from 21,144 to 25,218 (some 2026 listings show 24,686). **[CONFLICT on capacity figure]** [Soccer Stadium Digest](https://soccerstadiumdigest.com/providence-park-portland-timbers/); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Providence_Park)
- Downtown (Goose Hollow), on the MAX light rail; match tickets double as TriMet transit tickets in 2025-26. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Providence_Park); [Timbers.com](https://www.timbers.com/news/matchday-and-concert-tickets-at-providence-park-will-serve-as-trimet-tickets-in-2025-and-2026)
- Artificial turf (FieldTurf); described as the only soccer-specific MLS stadium without grass (as of 2023); turf replaced winter 2025-26. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Providence_Park); [Portland.gov](https://www.portland.gov/venues/overview/2024-2025-annual-report)
- Shared with Portland Thorns (NWSL, now separately owned), T2 and Portland Nitro (ultimate). No NFL tenant. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Providence_Park)
- Aug 2026: Paulson said he has put $145m of private money into the stadium and it needs continued investment; he framed the Blazers/Moda Center negotiations as "a wake-up call" for the region. No relocation or new-stadium plan announced for the Timbers. [OPB](https://www.opb.org/article/2026/08/18/portland-oregon-moda-center-sports-merritt-paulson/)

### Seattle Sounders FC
- Lumen Field opened 2002; owned by the Washington State Public Stadium Authority, operated by First & Goal (Seahawks-affiliated). NFL capacity 68,740; Sounders configuration 37,722, expandable to full bowl for big matches. [Wikipedia: Lumen Field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lumen_Field)
- Not soccer-specific; shared with the NFL Seahawks (and Reign FC). Artificial turf; temporary grass for the 2025 Club World Cup and 2026 World Cup (6 World Cup matches hosted). [Wikipedia: Lumen Field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lumen_Field)
- Pitch branded "Emerald Queen Casino Pitch at Lumen Field" (multi-year deal, in use by Aug 2025). [SoundersFC.com](https://www.soundersfc.com/news/sounders-fc-and-the-puyallup-tribe-of-indians-emerald-queen-casino-announce-new-); [OurSports Central photo 30 Aug 2025](https://www.oursportscentral.com/services/photo/photo/emerald-queen-casino-pitch-at-lumen-field-home-of-sounders-fc/n-67656)
- Lease expires 2032. Sept 2026 Annual Business Meeting: Hanauer called the situation "untenable"; a ~25,000-seat expandable stadium at the club's Longacres site in Renton is "very much on the table"; in a club survey 50% said they would never attend in Renton and 23.5% would attend less. [Sounder at Heart](https://www.sounderatheart.com/2026/09/key-takeaways-from-sounders-annual-business-meeting/)
- Oct 2025: club hired Moelis & Co. to find a large minority investor, citing stadium ambitions (valuation ~$825-900m). [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/soccer/story/_/id/46788164/seattle-sounders-seek-investment-fund-potential-soccer-stadium); [Sounder at Heart](https://www.sounderatheart.com/2025/10/sounders-say-they-are-seeking-new-investors/)
- Seahawks sold by the Allen estate in 2026 (Vinod Khosla bid accepted), making the Sounders a tenant of a new NFL owner's operator. [Sounder at Heart](https://www.sounderatheart.com/2026/07/how-the-seahawks-sale-might-impact-the-sounders-reign/)
- 2026 CONCACAF home matches moved to ONE Spokane Stadium because of World Cup preparations. [Wikipedia: 2026 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Seattle_Sounders_FC_season)

## 3. Values and community

### Portland Timbers
- Club charity: Timbers Community Fund / "Stand Together"; 2025 Stand Together Week had 500+ volunteers at 30+ sites. [Timbers.com](https://www.timbers.com/stand-together/community-fund); [search summary of Timbers.com video](https://www.timbers.com/video/a-look-back-at-the-2025-stand-together-week)

- Women's team: Paulson founded the Thorns (2013) but sold them after the abuse scandal; Bhathal family (RAJ Sports) bought them for an NWSL-record $63m, announced 3 Jan 2024. Timbers and Thorns now separately owned; a service agreement kept shared back-office staff for 2024. [Sportico](https://www.sportico.com/business/team-sales/2024/bhathal-portland-thorns-sale-1234761567/); [Equalizer](https://equalizersoccer.com/2024/01/03/portland-thorns-sale-to-bhathal-family-closes-at-63m/)

Minutes for homegrowns in the first team are limited (no league-wide comparison found). [Timbers.com (Izoita)](https://www.timbers.com/news/timbers-sign-homegrown-player-eric-izoita-to-first-team-contract); [Timbers.com (Jura)](https://www.timbers.com/news/timbers-sign-academy-player-sawyer-jura-to-homegrown-contract)

[Timbers.com](https://www.timbers.com/news/pride-night-comes-to-providence-park-on-june-8-with-limited-edition-scarf-special-performance); **[club ICE statement UNVERIFIED / not found]**

## 7. History and identity

### Portland Timbers

Folded 1982. [Wikipedia: Timbers 1975-82](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portland_Timbers_(1975%E2%80%931982)); [Wikipedia: Providence Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Providence_Park)

- 2025 marked the club's 50th anniversary; 2026 the stadium's 100th. [Wikipedia: 2025 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Portland_Timbers_season)
- Traditions: Timber Joey saws a log slice after each home goal (long-standing tradition; current status not rechecked). **[UNVERIFIED current]** [Wikipedia: Portland Timbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portland_Timbers)

### Seattle Sounders FC
- NASL Sounders 1974-83: Soccer Bowl finals 1977 and 1982 (both lost to the Cosmos); averaged 20,000+ 1975-82; played the first event at the Kingdome (1976). [Wikipedia: Sounders 1974-83](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle_Sounders_(1974%E2%80%931983))
- A-League/USL Sounders 1994-2008 (Hanauer owner from 2001); MLS since 2009 (led MLS attendance in year one). [Wikipedia: Seattle Sounders FC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle_Sounders_FC); [Wikipedia: Sounders supporters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle_Sounders_FC_supporters)
