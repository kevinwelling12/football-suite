Player Affinity

Your Affinity ratings, applied to players. Tap a player for the breakdown.

How it's scored

Six factors from 0 to 10, scored twice by independent researchers and averaged, weighted by your choices between 50 pairs of mystery players and 15 fair-fight pairs of real players: Team player 28%, Character 25%, Loyalty & bond 17%, Joy to watch 15%, Legacy 11%, Greatness 5%.

Character starts from the research score and shifts with your values (round 11): betting or crypto deals, refusing Pride or backing a right-leaning candidate pull it down; the rainbow armband, speaking out on racism, backing a left-leaning candidate, fair play and funding schools or clinics lift it. Round 12 adds conduct: drink-driving or partying, feuds, rows with fans or referees, taunting and pushing for moves through the media pull it down; championing the women's game, time with fans, honesty, refugees, the players' union, interests outside football and giving back to fan-owned or youth clubs lift it.

Connection is added on top, from your Affinity for each club a player has played for: up to +0.6 a season at clubs you rate 85 or more, nothing at 50, down to −0.6 at clubs you rate 20 or less (gains capped at +4, losses at −3). No club gets extra for being one you follow. Women's spells at European clubs you've only rated for the men's side count as neutral. +2 for US internationals. Playmakers and full-backs get +7, half that when it's a second position.

Penalties follow your baggage ranking, scaled by your choices: abuse allegations −50, racist abuse and match-fixing −39, violent conduct and authoritarian ambassadorships −19, tax fraud −14, doping −11, a forced transfer −8, a move to a Saudi, Gulf or Chinese money league −19 at 29 or younger, −11 at 30 to 33, −5.5 at 34 or older, joining a rival −5.5. Dropped or acquitted cases count 30%, apologies 60%. No cap. Past players aren't marked down for being before your time.
