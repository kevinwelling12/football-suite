## 2. Ground

### 1. FC Union Berlin
- Opened 1920 (Köpenick, beside the Wuhlheide woods); 22,012 capacity, 3,617 seats, rest terracing; Zingler: about 4,000 seats and 18,000 standing, "the least revenue per ticket in the Bundesliga". [Wikipedia: stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stadion_An_der_Alten_F%C3%B6rsterei); [Volksstimme/dpa](https://www.volksstimme.de/panorama/umbau-union-berlin-muss-langer-im-olympiastadion-bleiben-4313408)
- 2008-09 rebuild: over 2,300 supporters volunteered; club page cites 140,000+ volunteer hours; new main stand 2013 (€15m). [Wikipedia: stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stadion_An_der_Alten_F%C3%B6rsterei); [Wikipedia: club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1._FC_Union_Berlin)
- Owned by An der Alten Försterei Stadionbetriebs AG; fans/members bought shares in 2011 (€500 each) and again in 2025 (49,000+ new shares, ~€24.7m). [StadiumDB](https://stadiumdb.com/news/2025/02/germany_union_berlin_sells_49000_shares_a_new_chapter_for_alte_forsterei); [Berliner Kurier](https://www.berliner-kurier.de/union/1-fc-union-massenansturm-ab-dienstag-werden-die-stadionaktien-verteilt-li.2331520)
- Expansion: €190m (€99.5m invested to date). Demolition winter 2027-28, ~18 months build; home matches at the Olympiastadion (Hertha's ground) from early 2028 until the start of 2029-30. Delay due to tree-felling rules. Women will not play at the Olympiastadion; their venue is undecided. [fc-union-berlin.de AGM](https://www.fc-union-berlin.de/en/news/unions-agm-ARsCLS); [Volksstimme/dpa 3 Sep 2026](https://www.volksstimme.de/panorama/umbau-union-berlin-muss-langer-im-olympiastadion-bleiben-4313408)
- Capacity after expansion: 40,000 "long term" (dpa Sep 2026); 34,500 at opening (18,800 standing, 15,700 seated) per Berliner Zeitung Sep 2025; 32,500 per Wikipedia (Nov 2025); 40,500 in the 2024 plan. **[CONFLICT]** [Volksstimme/dpa](https://www.volksstimme.de/panorama/umbau-union-berlin-muss-langer-im-olympiastadion-bleiben-4313408); [Berliner Zeitung](https://www.berliner-zeitung.de/article/1-fc-union-berlin-dirk-zingler-erklaert-die-aenderung-der-alten-foersterei-2360074); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1._FC_Union_Berlin)
- Traditions at the ground: Weihnachtssingen (carols) since 2003 (89 fans) to ~28,500 (2015); 2014 "WM-Wohnzimmer", 800+ sofas on the pitch. [Wikipedia: stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stadion_An_der_Alten_F%C3%B6rsterei)

### Borussia Dortmund
- Westfalenstadion opened 2 April 1974 (built for the 1974 World Cup); 2006 World Cup venue. Capacity 81,365 league; ~65,829-66,099 all-seated for internationals **[CONFLICT, minor]**; standing allowed in the Champions League since 2022-23. [Wikipedia: Westfalenstadion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westfalenstadion)
- Ownership: built by the city, later sold to the club; sold to a property fund in the 2002 cash crisis; bought back in 2006 with a Morgan Stanley loan, repaid 2008. [Wikipedia: Westfalenstadion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westfalenstadion)
- Naming rights: Signal Iduna Park since Dec 2005, extended in 2022 to 2031. [Wikipedia: Westfalenstadion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westfalenstadion)
- About 3 km south of the city centre (geography). No move or major rebuild announced in anything found.

## 3. Ownership and governance

### 1. FC Union Berlin
- Registered association (e.V.); professional football not spun off (one of four Bundesliga e.V. clubs in 2022-23, with Freiburg, Mainz, Schalke). [Die falsche 9](https://www.diefalsche9.de/rechtsformen-der-profifussballabteilung-der-klubs-im-deutschen-profifussball-2022-23/)
- Members elect the supervisory board; the board appoints the president. Zingler president since 2004, reappointed to 2029; the presidium became full-time and gained its first two women. [news.de/dpa](https://www.news.de/sport/858743275/zingler-nun-hauptamtlicher-chef-von-union-berlin-1-fc-union-berlin-news-der-dpa-aktuell-zu-bundesliga/1/)
- 3 Sep 2026 AGM: chairman Thomas Koch's list (five incumbents plus one new member) elected; supervisory board and honorary council discharged by "clear majority". [fc-union-berlin.de](https://www.fc-union-berlin.de/en/news/unions-agm-ARsCLS)
- Finances 2025-26: revenue €197.62m, profit €0.41m, equity €8.50m; 2026-27 forecast €174.04m revenue, €0.34m profit. [fc-union-berlin.de](https://www.fc-union-berlin.de/en/news/unions-agm-ARsCLS)
- Zingler: club must make a transfer surplus every window; record sale of Ilyas Ansah to Hull City (up to ~€22m with bonuses, reported). [Volksstimme/dpa](https://www.volksstimme.de/panorama/zingler-verteidigt-transferpolitik-wir-sind-scheisse-geil-4313417)
- Conduct: in 2011 it emerged Zingler did his national service in the Felix Dzerzhinsky Guards Regiment (the Stasi's armed wing); he said he did not know of the Stasi link; he was also an SED member. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1._FC_Union_Berlin)
- DFL investor row (2023-24): Zingler backed a fresh vote ("without solid legitimacy there is no acceptance"); how Union voted in Dec 2023 not found. **[UNVERIFIED]** [Die falsche 9](https://www.diefalsche9.de/dfl-investor-zehn-klubs-offen-fuer-neue-abstimmung/)

### Borussia Dortmund
- Borussia Dortmund GmbH & Co. KGaA, first German club on the stock market (Oct 2000). The e.V. holds ~4.6-5.5% of shares but 100% of the general partner (Geschäftsführungs-GmbH), so it controls the company (50+1); the statutes require the e.V. to stay sole owner. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borussia_Dortmund); [bundesliga.com](https://www.bundesliga.com/en/bundesliga/news/50-1-fifty-plus-one-german-football-soccer-rule-explained-ownership-22832); [Rose & Partner](https://www.rosepartner.de/borussia-dortmund-verein-kg.html)
- Shareholders (Wikipedia): free float 67.24%, Bernd Geske 8.24%, Evonik 8.19%, Signal Iduna 5.98%, Puma 5.32%, e.V. 4.61% (date of snapshot unclear). **[UNVERIFIED current]** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borussia_Dortmund)
- Leadership: Watzke left the management board and was elected e.V. president on 23 Nov 2025, unopposed, with 59% of 4,244 votes; predecessor Reinhold Lunow withdrew after first planning to stand, citing member participation and the club's values code (he had turned against the Rheinmetall deal). Carsten Cramer is CEO; Lars Ricken managing director sport. [sport1](https://www.sport1.de/news/fussball/bundesliga/2025/11/bvb-wahlt-watzke-zum-prasidenten); [bvb.de](https://www.bvb.de/de/de/aktuelles/news/news.html/2025/11/23/Mitgliederversammlung-waehlt-Hans-Joachim-Watzke-zum-BVB-Praesidenten.html); [ZDF](https://www.zdfheute.de/sport/bundesliga-borussia-dortmund-watzke-lunow-100.html); [AP via barchart](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/36579410/dortmund-s-new-ceo-defends-sponsorship-deal-with-arms-manufacturer-rheinmetall)
- Sporting director Sebastian Kehl left 22 Mar 2026; Ole Book (ex-Elversberg) appointed in March. [sport1](https://www.sport1.de/news/fussball/bundesliga/2026/03/kehl-hammer-beim-bvb); [bundesliga.com](https://bundesliga.com/en/bundesliga/news/borussia-dortmund-2026-27-season-preview-challenge-bayern-kovac-nmecha-38849)
- Finances 2025-26: revenue €460.5m (-12.4%), net loss €21.7m; 2026-27 forecast break-even to +€10m. [Sportcal](https://www.sportcal.com/news/dortmund-increase-commercial-revenue-despite-steep-turnover-drop-in-2025-26/)
- Watzke as DFL supervisory board chair pushed the 2023 investor deal (passed 24-10-2, later abandoned after fan protests). [Flashscore](https://www.flashscore.de/news/fu-ball-2-bundesliga-chronologie-der-ereignisse-die-dfl-und-der-investorendeal-so-kam-es-zum-gro-en-knall/6ob3bIXj/)
- Aug 2026: club welcomed the Federal Cartel Office's 50+1 assessment ("members and fans belong at the heart of the club"). [bvb.de](https://www.bvb.de/de/en/news/news-overview/news.html/2026/8/12/Borussia-Dortmund-welcomes-the-Federal-Cartel-Offices-assessment.html)

## 4. Fans' say

### 1. FC Union Berlin
- Members vote at the AGM, elect the supervisory board, discharge boards; they do not elect the president directly. [news.de/dpa](https://www.news.de/sport/858743275/zingler-nun-hauptamtlicher-chef-von-union-berlin-1-fc-union-berlin-news-der-dpa-aktuell-zu-bundesliga/1/); [fc-union-berlin.de](https://www.fc-union-berlin.de/en/news/unions-agm-ARsCLS)
- Fan and Members Department (FuMA) regularly proposes a supervisory-board candidate who holds the fan brief; in 2026, fan clubs voted 66-0 (8 abstentions) to field their own candidate for the first time. Whether that candidate was elected: not stated. **[UNVERIFIED]** [fc-union-berlin.de](https://www.fc-union-berlin.de/en/news/fan-clubs-with-their-own-candidates-for-the-supervisory-board-election-KA5zcX)
- The 2007 anti-discrimination stadium rules came from a FuMA proposal. [fc-union-berlin.de](https://www.fc-union-berlin.de/en/engagement/engagement)
- Fans are stadium shareholders (see 2).

### Borussia Dortmund
- Members elect the e.V. president at the AGM (hybrid, 23-24 Nov 2025). [bvb.de](https://www.bvb.de/de/de/aktuelles/news/news.html/2025/11/23/Mitgliederversammlung-waehlt-Hans-Joachim-Watzke-zum-BVB-Praesidenten.html)
- Nov 2024: members present voted 556-247 (52 abstentions) to end the Rheinmetall deal as soon as possible and not extend it; non-binding; management kept the deal; 2025 shareholders rejected a motion not to discharge management over it. It expires after 2026-27. [Get Football News Germany](https://www.getfootballnewsgermany.com/2024/borussia-dortmund-members-vote-against-controversial-sponsorship/); [AP via barchart](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/36579410/dortmund-s-new-ceo-defends-sponsorship-deal-with-arms-manufacturer-rheinmetall); [Sportcal](https://www.sportcal.com/news/dortmund-increase-commercial-revenue-despite-steep-turnover-drop-in-2025-26/)

## 12. History and identity

### 1. FC Union Berlin
- Roots in FC Olympia Oberschöneweide (1906); current club founded 20 Jan 1966 in East Berlin; FDGB-Pokal 1968; rivalry with Stasi-backed BFC Dynamo (Wikipedia notes the "dissident" image is partly myth). [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1._FC_Union_Berlin)
- Licence refusals 1993-94, near-collapse 1997; 2004 "Bleed for Union": fans gave blood and donated the payments to secure the licence. 2001 cup final; first East Berlin club in the Bundesliga (2019). [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1._FC_Union_Berlin)
- "Eisern Union" cry; Weihnachtssingen; fan-built ground; 60th birthday Jan 2026. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1._FC_Union_Berlin)

### Borussia Dortmund
- Founded 19 Dec 1909 by 18 players (traditionally, young men leaving a Catholic parish youth group **[UNVERIFIED in this session]**); president replaced under the Nazis after refusing to join the party. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borussia_Dortmund)
- 1966 Cup Winners' Cup (first German European trophy, v Liverpool); 1997 Champions League and Intercontinental Cup; 2005 near-bankruptcy; 2017 team-bus bomb attack. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borussia_Dortmund)
- "You'll Never Walk Alone": first heard at the 1966 final; adopted at home via a 1996 cover by local band Pur Harmony; sung before kick-off. [bundesliga.com](https://www.bundesliga.com/en/bundesliga/news/why-do-borussia-dortmund-sing-youll-never-walk-alone-liverpool-29108); [liverpoolfc.com](https://www.liverpoolfc.com/news/first-team/214970-the-story-of-ynwa-and-borussiadortmund)
- 2026-27 home kit honours the Minister Stein colliery and the Dortmunder U (industrial heritage). [bvb.de](https://www.bvb.de/de/de/aktuelles/news/news.html/2026/5/8/Eine-Hommage-an-Dortmund-Das-BVB-Heimtrikot-fuer-die-Saison-2026-27.html)
