# The supporter archetype: Kevin

Who the Affinity ratings are built for. Drawn from his five clubs (Liverpool, Dortmund, Portland Timbers, Portland Thorns,
Sacramento Republic) and his own words (docs/preferences.md). scripts/archetype/live.py turns it into numbers.

1. **Belongs, doesn't shop.** He chose clubs that are their city's soul, with a famous singing end, rituals, a great
   ground and a crowd that sings for ninety minutes: the Kop, the Yellow Wall, the Timbers Army, the Rose City Riveters,
   the Tower Bridge Battalion. (Traits: city, terrace, rituals, ground.)
2. **Values first.** Inclusive, anti-racist fan cultures; clubs run for the club. None of his clubs is state, oligarch or
   private-equity owned, has a far-right fan identity, or fits the sportswashing mould. (Traits: conscience,
   stewardship; the shared floor: below the weakest of his clubs on any trait costs as if all five shared it.)
3. **Competitive, not dominant.** "Consistent success over time ... This does not mean domination." Tier 1, mid-table or
   better, with runs at trophies. (Trait: contender.)
4. **Not a plastic fan.** "Americans often get accused of being plastic fans ... I don't want that label." Global
   mega-brands and celebrity bandwagons lose a little: Deloitte Money League top 5 -3, 6-10 -2, 11-20 -1; Wrexham and
   Inter Miami -2.
5. **Rooted in Sacramento and his American teams.** Hometown club, Northern California, the Pacific Northwest. He backs
   the Kings, Giants, 49ers and Sharks: clubs owned by their owners gain (+1 to +2), clubs tied to their rivals lose
   (Rams, Dodgers, Lakers, A's under John Fisher, Golden Knights, Seahawks...: up to -5). (Trait: geography; scripts/affinity/big4.py.)
6. **A supporter's rivalries.** A Timbers and Thorns supporter doesn't warm to Seattle or Vancouver; a Dortmund supporter
   to Schalke or Bayern; a Liverpool supporter to Manchester United (Everton: the friendly derby, half). Bitter rivals
   -3, friendly -1.5. They can never top his own club in a shared league.
7. **Learning the game.** A newcomer since the 2026 World Cup, reading Gullit: drawn to front-foot, high-energy football
   and to the women's game through the Thorns. (Traits: intensity, women.)
8. **A father.** Player lists are topped by players he'd like his son to look up to: half the clubs they played for,
   half character (humility, work rate, time for fans, charity, speaking out, captaincy; incidents cost).
9. **Bandwidth.** He follows six competitions (Premier League, Bundesliga, Champions League, MLS, NWSL, USL) because those
   are where his clubs play; the app tracks only those.
