# Sanctions recount (one standard for every club and nation)

Count documented SANCTIONS and formal findings, not reputation. As of October 2026. Use the web: UEFA disciplinary
decisions (uefa.com "disciplinary"), FIFA disciplinary overviews, CONMEBOL/CONCACAF decisions, national FA / league
disciplinary results, and reliable press reports of those decisions. Same effort for every entity, famous or not.

For each entity give COUNTS (each separate sanction decision counts once; one match = one decision):
- discr_n: sanctions for racist, homophobic or other discriminatory behaviour by the club, its players as a club case, or
  its supporters, between July 2021 and October 2026. Include partial stadium closures and fines for discriminatory
  chants, banners or abuse. Do not count individual player bans for personal abuse (those are player facts).
- far_right: true if the club or federation is widely documented as tolerating an organised far-right or racist
  supporter identity now (not historical).
- viol_n: sanctions for supporter violence between July 2021 and October 2026: fighting, attacks on people, throwing
  objects at people, pitch invasions to attack. Do NOT count pyrotechnics-only, late kick-off, blocked stairways or
  "insufficient organisation" fines. Also count major documented violent incidents without a sanction (organised fights
  with arrests) and say so.
- deaths: true if supporter violence linked to the club caused a death since 2016.
- fin_n: financial-rule sanctions between July 2016 and October 2026: points deductions, UEFA settlement agreements or
  breaches of financial fair play / squad cost rules, league charges upheld, licensing sanctions. Do not count tax cases
  of individuals.
- notes: one neutral line per counted item (year, body, what), plus sources.

For nations: the national team's supporters and federation (UEFA/FIFA cases). fin_n = 0 for nations.
Never invent. If you can't find any sanction after a proper search, the count is 0.
