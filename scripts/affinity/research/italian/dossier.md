## 1. Supporters

## 3. Ownership and governance

### SSC Napoli
- Owned by Filmauro Srl, the De Laurentiis family film company; Aurelio De Laurentiis refounded the club in Sept 2004 after bankruptcy (Serie C). Football is now ~90% of Filmauro's revenue; no films produced in 2024-25. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SSC_Napoli); [ItaliaOggi / search summary](https://www.italiaoggi.it/marketing-e-media/media/filmauro-bilancio-in-rosso-per-de-laurentiis-e-conte-laddio-non-sarebbe-un-dramma-x2wj3qjt)
- Multi-club: Filmauro also owns SSC Bari (relegated to Serie C in 2026 after a play-out); Italian rules require the situation to end by 2028; sale talks (a US fund for 30%, Italian buyers) reported, nothing signed; the city of Bari set up a transparency committee. [QuiFinanza](https://quifinanza.it/sport-economy/bari-retrocessione-serie-c-vendita-societa/995059/); [Calcio e Finanza](https://www.calcioefinanza.it/?p=229890)
- Owner conduct: Nov 2025 De Laurentiis, CEO Andrea Chiavelli and the club were sent to trial in Rome for false accounting (2019-21 accounts; alleged fictitious capital gains on Manolas and Osimhen deals); trial set for 2 Dec 2026, but an April 2026 hearing raised a notification defect that could send it back to the preliminary judge (decision due 20 May, outcome not found). FIGC dropped its sporting case (2022). [Sky Sport](https://sport.sky.it/calcio/serie-a/2025/11/20/napoli-calcio-de-laurentiis-rinvio-a-giudizio-osimhen-news); [Calcio e Finanza](https://www.calcioefinanza.it/2026/04/15/processo-de-laurentiis-falso-in-bilancio-napoli/) **[status UNVERIFIED]**
- Owner conduct: called 10-15% of the crowd ultras, "mostly outlaws" (2025); a court ordered him to pay police costs at the stadium (reported). Sept 2023 club TikTok mocked Osimhen's missed penalty ("I'm a coconut"), criticised as racist; the player's agent threatened legal action. [Open](https://www.open.online/2025/11/24/napoli-ultras-manifesti-de-laurentiis-saviano-parassita/); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SSC_Napoli)
- Coach turnover: Allegri is the 13th coach of the De Laurentiis era. [Open](https://www.open.online/2026/07/03/napoli-allegri-ufficiale-tredicesimo-allenatore-de-laurentiis-cambi-serie-a/)
- Finances 2024-25: revenue €290.8m, loss €21.4m (after +€63m), covered from €216.6m of reserves; equity €190.2m; cash €174.4m, net financial position positive (net cash) €137m; bank debt €37m (SACE loans to 2027). [Calcio e Finanza](https://www.calcioefinanza.it/?p=209380); [Calcio e Finanza](https://www.calcioefinanza.it/?p=210583)

- Bought by Rocco Commisso (Italian-born US founder of cable operator Mediacom) in June 2019 for ~€160m. He died 16 Jan 2026 (76); son Giuseppe Commisso is president; family holds 100% via JMCC Corp and Columbia Soccer Ventures; family and executives say the club is not for sale (Jan and Apr 2026). [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ACF_Fiorentina); [LaPresse](https://www.lapresse.it/calcio/2026/01/17/rocco-commisso-morto-a-76-anni-il-presidente-della-fiorentina/); [Fantacalcio](https://www.fantacalcio.it/news/calcio-italia/22_01_2026/fiorentina-la-famiglia-commisso-restera-proprietaria-487900); [TMW](https://www.tuttomercatoweb.com/serie-a/paratici-commisso-non-vuole-vendere-la-fiorentina-speriamo-di-tenere-kean-fagioli-da-barca-2221639)
- Investment: ~€478m from summer 2019 to June 2025 incl. holding payments and Mediacom sponsorship. [Calcio e Finanza](https://www.calcioefinanza.it/?p=207825)

[Calcio e Finanza](https://www.calcioefinanza.it/2025/11/03/fiorentina-bilancio-2025-fatturato-perdita-debiti/)
- Management: Alessandro Ferrari director general; Fabio Paratici head of football since Jan-Feb 2026 (to 2030); Paratici served a 30-month FIGC ban (extended worldwide by FIFA) from the Juventus capital-gains case and is reported indicted in the related criminal case. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fabio_Paratici); [Calcio e Finanza](https://www.calcioefinanza.it/?p=222782) **[criminal status UNVERIFIED]**

## 12. History and identity

### SSC Napoli
- Roots in 1905 (Naples Foot-Ball Club, founded by an English sailor); AC Napoli formed 1926. Bankrupt Aug 2004; refounded by De Laurentiis in Serie C, drawing a third-tier record ~51,000 crowd. The Maradona era (titles 1987, 1990, UEFA Cup 1989) made the club a symbol of the South; ground renamed for him in 2020. Centenary season 2026-27. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SSC_Napoli); research/out b07; [Calcio e Finanza](https://www.calcioefinanza.it/?p=230641)

### ACF Fiorentina
- Founded 29 Aug 1926 by aristocrat Luigi Ridolfi, merging two clubs to give Florence a rival to the northern powers. Bankrupt 2002; refounded by Diego Della Valle in Serie C2, admitted to Serie B in 2003 and bought back the name. Relegated in Calciopoli (2006, later commuted to a points penalty). Centenary Aug 2026. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ACF_Fiorentina)

### Atalanta BC
- Founded 17 Oct 1907 by secondary-school students in Bergamo; four Serie B titles and many top-flight/second-tier yo-yo spells; 16th straight Serie A season now. The 2016-25 Gasperini era took a provincial club to the Champions League and a European trophy; the Curva Nord and Curva Sud are named for a fan and a player who died young. Annual "Festa della Dea" fan festival. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atalanta_BC); [Wikipedia 2026-27](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026%E2%80%9327_Atalanta_BC_season)
