# Association ties: review list (74 found; proposed: drop low-confidence and heritage -> 56)


## strong

- Houston Dynamo FC / Houston Dash [shared supporters, high]: The Dash were created by the Dynamo in 2013 with the Dynamo's orange and black colours and share Shell Energy Stadium and ownership; Dynamo supporters' groups such as The Surge explicitly support the Dash too.
- Louisville City FC / Racing Louisville [shared supporters, high]: Racing Louisville was founded by LouCity's ownership and shares Lynn Family Stadium; The Coopers supporters' group represents and leads support for both clubs from the Estopinal End, and the clubs run a joint '502 Crew' supporter-section programme.
- Orlando City / Orlando Pride [shared supporters, high]: Men's and women's teams of the same club: same ownership, same Inter&Co Stadium, and The Wall supporters' section (The Ruckus, Iron Lion Firm) backs both teams.
- Portland Timbers / Portland Thorns [shared supporters, high]: Both play at Providence Park; the Thorns' Rose City Riveters were founded by Timbers Army members and both groups sit under the 107 Independent Supporters Trust. Ownership of the two clubs has been separate since the 2024 sale of the Thorns, but the supporter base remains shared.
- Real Salt Lake / Utah Royals [shared supporters, medium]: The Royals were relaunched in 2024 by Real Salt Lake's owners, are run within the RSL organisation and play at America First Field ('the RioT'); supporters' groups such as Los Caballeros Reales back both teams. Name and colours differ, so the identity is only partly shared.

## medium

- 1. FC Köln / Borussia Dortmund [fan friendship, medium]: Ultras-level friendship (Wilde Horde and The Unity) since Köln's 2011 win over Leverkusen handed Dortmund the title; listed among current Bundesliga fan friendships.
- AC Milan / Sevilla [fan friendship, medium]: Milan's Curva Sud lists Sevilla among its current international twinnings (from around 2014), alongside CSKA Sofia and Partizan.
- Athletic Club / Osasuna [fan friendship, medium]: Herri Norte Taldea and Indar Gorri are twinned in the Basque left-nationalist ultras network.
- Athletic Club / Real Sociedad [fan friendship, medium]: Although the clubs contest the Basque derby, Herri Norte Taldea and Real Sociedad's ultras (Bultzada / Real Sociedad Firm) are twinned in the same Basque ultras network.
- Athletic Club / Alavés [fan friendship, medium]: Herri Norte Taldea and Alavés's Iraultza 1921 are twinned in the Basque left-nationalist ultras network.
- Athletic Club / Celta Vigo [fan friendship, medium]: Herri Norte Taldea is twinned with Celtarras, Celta's left-wing, pro-independence ultras.
- Borussia Mönchengladbach / Schalke 04 [fan friendship, medium]: New friendship officially listed by Schalke's ultras in 2025 after reciprocal visits, built on shared dislike of Köln and Dortmund.
- Eintracht Frankfurt / Atalanta [fan friendship, high]: Ultras twinning since a chance meeting on a train in January 2000; renewed with mass visits to Bergamo in 2016 and 2022 and one of the best-known cross-border gemellaggi.
- Fiorentina / Torino [fan friendship, high]: Twinning since about 1969-73, rooted in Fiorentina's closeness to Torino after Superga and a shared rivalry with Juventus; still active.
- Fiorentina / Sporting CP [fan friendship, high]: Juventude Leonina and Directivo Ultras XXI are twinned with Fiorentina ultras; Juve Leo wore black at the 2018 Lisbon derby to honour a murdered Fiorentina fan.
- Genoa / Torino [fan friendship, high]: Historic twinning born in memory of Gigi Meroni; strained in 2009 and partly repaired in 2012; Genoa's most valued gemellaggio.
- Genoa / Marseille [fan friendship, medium]: Genoa supporters are twinned with Marseille's South Winners only (Marseille's Commando Ultra is instead twinned with Sampdoria).
- Inter Milan / Lazio [fan friendship, high]: One of Italy's most solid ultras twinnings (Boys San / Curva Nord Inter and Lazio's Curva Nord), dating from the late 1980s; both far-right-leaning curve.
- Inter Milan / Nice [fan friendship, high]: Official twinning since 2013; Nice ultras regularly appear at San Siro and sided with Inter ultras in the December 2018 clashes with Napoli.
- Inter Milan / PSV Eindhoven [fan friendship, medium]: Twinning with PSV's Ehv-Hools and Boys '91 made official in 2022-23.
- Lazio / Real Madrid [fan friendship, medium]: Irriducibili and Ultras Sur twinned in 2001 ('divided by teams, united by mentality'), reinforced in 2007-08. Ultras Sur have been barred from the Bernabéu since 2014.
- Lazio / Espanyol [fan friendship, high]: Twinning between the Irriducibili and Espanyol's Brigadas Blanquiazules, both far-right, built on shared hostility to Barcelona; the Spanish banner often appears in Lazio's Curva Nord.
- Lille / Nice [fan friendship, high]: Lille's Dogues Virage Est and Nice's Brigade Sud have been friends since the late 1990s; Lille shared its section with Nice ultras after the Brigade Sud was dissolved.
- Liverpool / Borussia Dortmund [ritual, high]: Both clubs' supporters sing You'll Never Walk Alone as their anthem; at the 2016 Europa League tie on the Hillsborough anniversary the two crowds sang it together, which won both sets of fans the FIFA Fan Award.
- Liverpool / Feyenoord [ritual, medium]: Feyenoord adopted You'll Never Walk Alone (Lee Towers' 1976 version) as a club anthem; Feyenoord's travelling fans sang it in tribute to Arne Slot when he moved to Liverpool.
- Liverpool / Mainz 05 [ritual, medium]: Mainz 05 sing You'll Never Walk Alone before home matches (one of only two German clubs, with Dortmund); the link is often highlighted through Jurgen Klopp, who managed both clubs.
- Liverpool / Borussia Mönchengladbach [fan friendship, high]: A long-standing fan friendship dating from the 1973, 1977 and 1978 European meetings, strengthened when Gladbach fans raised money for Hillsborough families; supporters still visit each other's grounds.
- Lyon / Real Madrid [fan friendship, medium]: Lyon's Bad Gones declare a friendship with Real Madrid's Ultras Sur, who attended the Bad Gones' 35th anniversary match in 2022.
- Marseille / AEK Athens [fan friendship, high]: Commando Ultra '84 and AEK's Original 21 have been friends for about 35 years (with Livorno, the 'triangle of brotherhood'); AEK's 1989 visit shaped Marseille's ultras.
- Napoli / Borussia Dortmund [fan friendship, high]: Napoli's curve are twinned with Dortmund ultras, a link brokered via Catania; Dortmund ultras urged fans to respect the friendship when travelling to Naples.
- Napoli / Feyenoord [fan friendship, medium]: Listed as a Napoli twinning; Napoli ultras invited Feyenoord's hard core to Naples in 2023.
- Napoli / Paris Saint-Germain [fan friendship, high]: Twinning since 2017 between Napoli's Curva B (Ultras 1972) and PSG's K-Soce Team (Collectif Ultras Paris), with banners shown at each other's grounds.
- Osasuna / Rayo Vallecano [fan friendship, medium]: Indar Gorri (Osasuna) and Bukaneros (Rayo) are twinned.
- Osasuna / Deportivo La Coruña [fan friendship, medium]: Indar Gorri (Osasuna) and Riazor Blues (Deportivo) are twinned.
- Osasuna / Real Sociedad [fan friendship, low] (drop: low confidence): Indar Gorri is allied and twinned with Real Sociedad Firm in the Basque left-nationalist ultras network.
- Osasuna / Alavés [fan friendship, low] (drop: low confidence): Indar Gorri is allied and twinned with Alavés's Iraultza 1921 in the Basque left-nationalist ultras network.
- Portland Timbers / Chicago Fire FC [fan friendship, medium]: Section 8 Chicago and the Timbers Army have had a friendship since about 2004, with members visiting each other's matches, joint trips such as the 2009 'Allied Invasion' of Seattle, and supporters' matches between the groups; Section 8 was a model for the 107IST.
- Real Madrid / Espanyol [fan friendship, high]: Ultras Sur and Espanyol's Brigadas Blanquiazules have had a strong far-right alliance since 1988.
- Roma / Atlético Madrid [fan friendship, high]: Official twinning between Roma's curva and Frente Atlético.
- Sevilla / Marseille [fan friendship, medium]: Biris Norte are listed as twinned with Marseille's Commando Ultra '84, within the left-wing ultras network.
- Sevilla / Deportivo La Coruña [fan friendship, high]: Biris Norte and Riazor Blues, both left-wing, are officially twinned and display banners together when the teams meet.
- Sevilla / Rayo Vallecano [fan friendship, medium]: Biris Norte are twinned with Rayo's Bukaneros in the left-wing ultras network.
- Sunderland / Feyenoord [fan friendship, high]: Sections of both fan bases share a friendship that began when Wearside shipbuilders worked in Rotterdam in the 1970s and 80s; a 'Feyenoord Mackems' branch travels to the Stadium of Light with its own flag.
- Sunderland / Norwich City [fan friendship, high]: After the 1985 League Cup final, where Sunderland fans applauded Norwich's lap of honour and the fans swapped scarves, the clubs have played for a 'Friendship Trophy' whenever they meet.
- Union Berlin / Borussia Mönchengladbach [fan friendship, medium]: Union's Hammerhearts and Gladbach's Sottocultura ultras have had an official group friendship since 2018 (contacts since 2009); the groups describe it as a group rather than club-wide friendship.
- West Ham United / Lazio [fan friendship, high]: Lazio's ultras count West Ham among their main international friendships, which grew from fan contacts and the shared love of Paolo Di Canio; supporters attend each other's matches.

## light

- Deportivo La Coruña / Rayo Vallecano [fan friendship, medium]: Riazor Blues and Bukaneros keep good relations, short of a formal twinning.

- San Diego FC / San Diego Wave [shared supporters, low] (drop: low confidence): Both clubs play at Snapdragon Stadium; they are separately owned with separate identities and supporters' groups.
- San Jose Earthquakes / Bay FC [shared supporters, low] (drop: low confidence): Bay FC signed a five-year deal to play at the Earthquakes' PayPal Park, with dedicated facilities there; the clubs are separately owned and branded.

- Southampton / Athletic Club [heritage, high] (drop: heritage): In 1909 Athletic's Juan Elorduy bought 50 Southampton shirts in Southampton, and Athletic have worn red-and-white stripes since January 1910; the clubs have marked the link with friendlies.
